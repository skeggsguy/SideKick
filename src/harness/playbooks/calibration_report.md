Weekly approval-calibration check.

1. Call calibration_report (default 90-day window).
2. Write a report of AT MOST 100 words:
   - How well forecasts tracked verdicts (call out any tool with a gap over
     20 percentage points).
   - Any measured allowlist candidates, verbatim from the tool output, with a
     one-line recommendation ("consider promoting X in Tools-Security").
   - If there's no data yet, say so in one sentence and stop.

Do not promote anything yourself — allowlisting is a human action in the
Tools-Security screen.
